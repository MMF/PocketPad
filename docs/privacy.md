# Privacy Policy for PocketPad

**Effective date:** November 12, 2026

PocketPad ("the app") is an open-source Android app published by MFouadDev. It
lets you use your phone as a Bluetooth mouse and trackpad for a computer. The
phone connects as a standard Bluetooth input device (Bluetooth HID), so no
software is installed on the computer.

## Summary

PocketPad does not collect, store on any server, sell, or share any personal data.
The app has no internet access, no advertising, no analytics, and no user accounts.

## What the app accesses and why

- **Bluetooth:** used to make your phone visible to, pair with, and connect to the
  computers you choose, and to send pointer movement, clicks, scrolling, and
  shortcut commands to the connected computer. This information goes directly
  from your phone to that computer over Bluetooth and nowhere else.
- **Touch input:** your finger movements on the trackpad screen are converted into
  mouse actions in real time. They are not recorded or stored.
- **Notifications:** used to show that the trackpad is active and to let you stop
  it.
- **Vibration:** used for optional click feedback.

## Data stored on your phone

The app saves the following on your device only:

- Your settings (for example sensitivity and scroll direction).
- The names and Bluetooth addresses of computers you have paired with, and the
  profile you chose for each one.
- An optional diagnostics log, which is off by default. It contains technical
  events such as connection status and the commands sent to the computer. It
  stays on your phone unless you choose to copy or share it yourself.

You can delete all of this at any time by clearing the app's data in Android
settings or by uninstalling the app.

## Data shared with third parties

None. The app does not send any data to the developer or to any third party, and
it contains no third-party advertising or analytics software.

## Children

The app is not directed at children and does not collect personal data from
anyone.
